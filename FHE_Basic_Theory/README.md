FHE Theory

This directory contains notes on the fundamental concepts and mathematical foundations of Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE), mainly focusing on the BFV and CKKS schemes.

The contents are intended to provide an intuitive and implementation-oriented understanding of FHE, including topics such as:

Polynomial rings and RNS representation

BFV scheme

CKKS scheme

Encoding and decoding

Homomorphic addition and multiplication

Relinearization and key switching

Modulus switching and rescaling

If you are looking for a more in-depth or mathematically rigorous explanation, I strongly recommend reading the original papers and related literature rather than relying solely on these notes.
